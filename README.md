# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of e-commerce sales data using Power BI. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators, evaluate sales performance across regions and product categories, identify profitability trends, and understand customer purchasing behavior through an interactive and data-driven reporting solution.


## Business Problem

Organizations generate large volumes of sales data every day. However, without proper reporting and analysis, identifying profitable products, monitoring regional performance, and understanding customer behavior becomes challenging.

This project addresses these challenges by providing a centralized dashboard that enables business users to monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.


## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming regions.
- Evaluate category-wise and sub-category-wise business performance.
- Monitor monthly profit trends and business growth patterns.
- Identify high-value customers contributing significantly to revenue.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Support business decision-making through interactive visualizations.


## Business Questions Answered

- Which states generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories contribute the most to sales volume?
- Which sub-categories are the most profitable?
- How does profit vary across different months?
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- Which payment methods are preferred by customers?
- Which areas require business improvement and optimization?


## Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization


## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following business metrics:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value (AOV)


## Dashboard Features

### Executive Summary
Provides a high-level overview of business performance through KPI cards and summary metrics.

### Regional Performance Analysis
Analyzes revenue distribution across different states to identify key revenue-generating markets.

### Category Performance Analysis
Evaluates sales contribution across product categories and identifies growth opportunities.

### Profitability Analysis
Tracks monthly profit trends and highlights profitable and loss-making business segments.

### Customer Analysis
Identifies top customers based on revenue contribution and purchasing activity.

### Payment Method Analysis
Provides insights into customer payment preferences and transaction behavior.

### Interactive Filtering
Allows users to filter data dynamically by:
- State
- Quarter

for deeper business analysis.


## Dashboard Preview

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales performance, profitability trends, customer behavior, payment preferences, and regional performance through dynamic visualizations and filters.

## Key Insights

- Revenue generation is concentrated among a limited number of high-performing states.
- Profitability fluctuates across different months, indicating seasonal or operational variations.
- Certain product sub-categories contribute significantly more profit than others.
- A small group of customers accounts for a substantial share of total revenue.
- Customer payment preferences reveal purchasing behavior and transaction trends.


## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. Increase marketing and sales efforts in high-performing regions to maximize revenue growth.
2. Investigate low-profit periods to identify operational, pricing, or cost-related issues.
3. Focus inventory planning and promotional activities on high-profit product categories.
4. Develop customer retention strategies for high-value customers.
5. Improve performance in underperforming regions through targeted business initiatives.


## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates the practical application of Business Intelligence and Data Analytics techniques to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

By combining KPI tracking, data visualization, and business analysis, the dashboard provides stakeholders with a powerful tool for monitoring performance, identifying opportunities, and supporting informed decision-making.
